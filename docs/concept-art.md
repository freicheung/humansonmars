# Concept art brief

Concept images replace the drawn backgrounds on full-screen panels. Every image must look like part of one series,
so use the shared style block in every prompt and only change the scene line.

## Shared style block (paste into every prompt)

> Cinematic wide-angle photograph-style concept art of Mars, 16:9, ultra high resolution. Palette limited to deep
> black, burnt orange (#C1440E), rust and warm sand, with one cool accent at most. Low sun, long shadows, fine
> airborne dust, film grain. Vast scale, small human elements, quiet and awe-inspiring mood. Large calm area of dark
> sky or ground on the LEFT third for headline text. No text, no logos, no flags, no brand markings, no recognisable
> real spacecraft, no recognisable people or faces.

## Scenes

| File name | Panel | Scene line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hero-mars-rise.webp` | Home hero | Mars seen from orbit, the planet rising from the lower right, thin glowing atmosphere limb, star field. |
| `calling-home.webp` | Home 01, message delay tool | A lone figure in a pressure suit on a ridge at dusk, looking up at a tiny bright blue dot (Earth) in the sky. |
| `gravity-horizon.webp` | Home 02, gravity tool | Rolling dunes and a rocky plain at sunset, two suited explorers mid-stride leaving long shadows. |
| `martian-year.webp` | Home 03, Mars age tool | Time-lapse feel: dusk sky with two small moons, layered cliffs, star trails above. |
| `habitat-dusk.webp` | Home 04 | A small cluster of half-buried sealed habitat domes glowing warmly at dusk, a rover parked nearby. |

## Rules

- Export at 2400 px wide or more, WebP or JPEG, and place the file in `src/assets/concept/`.
- Every concept image is shown with the label "Concept art · AI-generated". Do not remove it.
- Never use a generated image to illustrate a real mission or news event.
- Keep the left third calm; the site places headlines there.
